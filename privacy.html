TokMark

Privacy Policy & Station Security

Effective & Last Revised: September 2026 | Hospital Operating Protocol

1. What TokMark Does (Our Core Purpose)

TokMark is an ultra-fast, real-time OPD queue orchestration and Smart TV token broadcast layer designed exclusively for clinics and hospitals. We do not store sensitive electronic health records (EHR) or patient clinical histories. Our architecture is built strictly to manage live turn sequences, queue notifications, and room assignments.

2. Information We Collect

  • Station Administrative Data: Hospital or clinic name, branch city, primary administrator phone number, and official verified email address.
  • Queue Data: Token sequence numbers, patient names and mobile numbers, queue timestamps, and assigned room IDs. Automatic expiry is not implemented.
  • Staff Profiles: Doctor names, email addresses, departments, and room assignments. PIN sign-in is disabled; legacy PIN fields must be removed through a controlled migration.

3. Doctor Departure & Station Decoupling Critical

Secure doctor access is not currently available. Do not use the doctor station with real patient data until Firebase Auth identities and server-enforced doctor roles are provisioned.

  • Browser storage and frontend checks are not access controls; the doctor desk now requires a Firebase Auth UID linked to the canonical doctor profile.
  • Administrative profile changes do not replace backend account revocation or forced session revocation.
  • Do not enter clinical notes; TokSpot is an OPD queue product, not an EHR.

4. Data Security & Storage

Data is stored in Firebase Firestore. This repository contains no Firestore rules or trusted backend, so tenant isolation, role authorization, audit logging, and retention are not yet production-enforced. Firestore's platform encryption does not replace those controls. Do not store real patient data until they are deployed and tested.
