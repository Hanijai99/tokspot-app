TokSpot Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

TokSpot collects minimal required information solely to facilitate outpatient (OPD) queueing and doctor consultation calling:

  • Patient Details: Name and contact number provided during walk-in or self-booking for queue SMS notifications and display board identification.
  • Doctor & Staff Data: Doctor name, room/counter designation, email address, and hashed authentication PINs.
  • Hospital Information: Hospital name, station branch city, and unique routing identifiers (Hospital Code).

2. How Data Is Used

All collected queue tokens are utilized exclusively for real-time synchronization between waiting room smart TVs, receptionist desks, and doctor consultation rooms via secure Google Firestore cloud databases.

3. Data Security & Encryption

Doctor PIN credentials are encrypted and hashed before cloud persistence. We do not sell, rent, or lease patient phone numbers or clinical consultation notes to third parties or advertising brokers.

4. Retention & Auto-Expiry

OPD daily token records are associated with that business date and are archived or marked expired upon the close of the daily clinical shift by hospital administrators.

5. Contact Us

For administrative data inquiries or hospital profile de-registration, contact your healthcare provider desk or write to our support desk.
